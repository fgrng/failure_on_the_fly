## Textfassung

> **Introduction**
>
> A student says something and the teacher has a few seconds. The answer may be a slip, or it may be
> the visible end of a rule the student has been applying for months. The question the teacher asks
> next either brings that rule out or does not, and the lesson moves on either way.
>
> Learning to do this is part of learning to teach mathematics. Teacher education brings practice
> into a course through vignettes, which show a classroom situation without its time pressure. What
> a vignette cannot do is answer. It was assembled before the pre-service teachers arrived and stays
> as it is whatever they ask, so the half of the work that consists of asking is not there to be
> practised. Role play answers, but then the student is whatever the peer playing the role makes of
> it.
>
> We built Failure on the Fly for that missing half. Pre-service teachers put their own questions to
> a simulated student, a language model instructed to apply one error pattern, and the vignette
> settles which pattern before anyone starts talking.
>
> Section 2 sets out what on-the-fly assessment requires, which is that the teacher bring a
> student's thinking out rather than collect it. Section 3 examines what case work can and cannot
> offer for that, and Section 4 whether a simulated student holds an error under questioning.
> Section 5 presents the application with one worked vignette from descriptive statistics, Section 6
> the research question and Section 7 the design.
>
> This is a theoretical and developmental paper. It offers an argument, a tool derived from it and a
> study now under way, without findings yet. Data collection runs from autumn to spring, and we will
> report the first of them at the conference.

Abstract und noch einmal am Ende der Einleitung. Reviewkriterium 4 lautet „Statements and discussion

anderes Versprechen gibt. Das Versprechen lautet hier wörtlich „an argument, a tool derived from it
and a study now under way", und „without findings yet" sagt das Fehlende unumwunden. Der Satz zur
